## Purpose

This file documents scientific observations and discoveries made by equipment left on or around the Moon and Mars. Each entry must distinguish:
- **Observation:** what the instrument directly measured or imaged.
- **Evidence:** the data supporting the observation.
- **Interpretation:** what scientists infer from the evidence.
- **Significance:** why the result matters.

Do not present an interpretation as a direct measurement.

## Starter research topics

### 1. Opportunity — evidence of past water at Meridiani Planum
Research Opportunity's observations of sedimentary rocks and hematite-rich spherules (“blueberries”). Explain what was observed, which instruments contributed, and why scientists interpreted the geology as evidence of past water-related processes. Avoid simplifying the result to “Mars had oceans everywhere”; describe the specific evidence and environmental interpretation in the cited studies.

Questions to answer:
- What are hematite-rich spherules?
- Which instruments and observations characterized them?
- What geological context supports the aqueous interpretation?
- What do the findings establish, and what do they not establish?

### 2. Spirit — evidence of water-related alteration
Research Spirit's observations of rocks and soils in Gusev Crater, including findings from the Columbia Hills. Investigate the evidence for past water-related alteration and the distinction between volcanic, impact, and hydrothermal processes.

Questions to answer:
- Which rocks and soil targets were studied?
- Which instruments analyzed them?
- What mineralogical or chemical evidence was reported?
- How did later discoveries refine the original interpretation?

### 3. Sojourner — early in-situ rover geology
Research how Sojourner examined nearby rocks and soil at the Pathfinder site. Explain what its Alpha Proton X-ray Spectrometer (APXS) measured and how the rover demonstrated mobile surface science.

Questions to answer:
- What was the rover's scientific payload?
- What types of rocks and soil were examined?
- What did the measurements reveal about local composition?
- What were the limitations of a short-range technology demonstration?
